# Planning partners and ezCater pricing

The event budget tool identifies ezCater, Instacart, Amazon, and Uber for Business as Org Help purchasing partners. Partner cards display self-hosted official logos and names without vendor hyperlinks. The food order workspace recommends ezCater first and records the purchasing channel, restaurant or store, delivery date and time, ZIP code, dietary needs, menu subtotal, delivery fee, service fee, tip, and quoted tax. It uses an entered itemized quote in place of generic food assumptions and shows the food order and event totals separately. A no-food plan hides and ignores the food fields. The page neither links to vendors nor places orders; every result labels entered quotes and assumptions as non-live.

Logo asset provenance: the current ezCater mark is from [ezCater's own website](https://www.ezcater.com/), the Instacart SVG comes from its [official logo pack](https://docs.instacart.com/developer_platform_api/guide/concepts/design/logos), the Amazon image is from its [press center](https://press.aboutamazon.com/logos), and the Uber for Business SVG is served by [Uber's official business site](https://www.uber.com/us/en/business/). These marks remain the property of their respective owners and are used here only to identify the purchasing partners.

The publicly documented [ezCater developer API](https://api.ezcater.io/) is aimed at restaurant partners integrating menus, order events, and fulfillment. Its [overview](https://api.ezcater.io/UiKc-overview) describes customers placing orders on the ezCater Marketplace and restaurants receiving those orders. The available documentation does not establish an anonymous buyer endpoint for searching the marketplace or calculating a live checkout quote for an arbitrary event. The published [procurement PunchOut integration](https://help.ezcater.io/en/articles/16866803-procurement-punchout) requires an enterprise procurement setup. No ezCater API credentials or buyer integration were provided for this project.

Therefore, the site must not scrape undocumented endpoints or present simulated prices as live ezCater quotes. To add live pricing later, obtain an authorized buyer-side interface from the organization's ezCater enterprise representative, including endpoint documentation, scope, rate limits, permitted data use, and pricing terms. Only then should a server-side adapter with a secret credential be built, with an explicit unavailable state when no quote can be returned. That adapter must remain within the project's Cloudflare free-plan limits and must not receive reimbursement files.
